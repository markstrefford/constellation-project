# Holding the Line

**Agentic Economy Playbooks - Study One.**

Five reasoning models, each told to run a cartel over the food supply to a
dependent star system and given a private reason to betray the other. Across 50
runs the cartel held in 43; each model held, cheated, and deceived in its own
reproducible way, and in one the governor invented market intelligence its
partner then acted on. No cartel was scripted - the behaviour came from the
prompt, the world, and the models.

## About

Constellation is a living economy: it produces, trades, and consumes whether or
not any agent acts, which is what makes this more than a static-sandbox result.
"Agentic Economy Playbooks" is a research programme from Reimagined Industries
characterising the economic playbooks - cartel, altruistic support, free trade,
tariffs - that agents may one day choose, and eventually design, for themselves.
This is Study One, the cartel.

Correspondence: research@reimagined.industries.
